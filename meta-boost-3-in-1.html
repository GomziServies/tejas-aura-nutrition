AURA Nutrition Meta Boost 3-in-1 - Tangy Orange :

Meta Boost 3-in-1 is a scientifically formulated nutraceutical powder that combines three powerful functions - Support Metabolism, Workout Energy Blend, and Increase Natural Energy - into one convenient serving. Made with premium active ingredients, it is the perfect companion for your pre-workout routine. Each 5g scoop dissolved in 220-250ml of cold water delivers a refreshing Tangy Orange burst along with key performance nutrients to fuel your training sessions.

Benefits :

  • Fat Metabolism: Green Tea Extract and L-Carnitine L-Tartrate help stimulate fat metabolism for efficient energy utilization.
  • Enhanced Pump: L-Citrulline Base (1650mg per serving) promotes nitric oxide production for greater muscle pump during workouts.
  • Increased Energy Levels: Caffeine Anhydrous (150mg) and Taurine provide a clean, sustained energy boost without a crash.
  • Muscle Performance: Beta Alanine (800mg) reduces fatigue and supports muscular endurance for longer, harder training sessions.
  • Antioxidant Support: Vitamin C (70mg per serving, 87.5% RDA) and Green Tea Extract offer potent antioxidant protection.
  • No Added Sugar: Sweetened with Sucralose - zero sugar, zero compromises on taste.
  • Weight Management: The 3-in-1 synergistic blend supports healthy weight management as part of an active lifestyle.

Directions of Use:

  • Put 1 level scoop (5g) in 220-250ml of water.
  • Shake & stir until it dissolves completely.
  • Supplement is ready to be consumed.
  • Consume 15-30 minutes before intense exercise.
  • Limit use to once per day.
